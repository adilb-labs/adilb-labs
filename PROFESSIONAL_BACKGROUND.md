## Why this matters in software and AI work

Construction and engineering workflows have a useful property: mistakes are not abstract. A wrong quantity, threshold, price basis, remainder classification, or document value can create real cost.

That practical background informs how I approach software and AI systems today:

1. define the source of truth;
2. make calculation rules explicit;
3. test boundary cases;
4. separate verified facts from assumptions;
5. preserve provenance;
6. automate only after the rule is understood;
7. qualify the result before scaling it.

The objective is not to replace engineering judgment with automation.

The objective is to make high-value technical workflows **more explicit, testable, reproducible, and efficient**.
